# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 26 October 2026**

Self Custody Labs counts page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, sets no cookies, and has no accounts, forms, comments or newsletter. Nothing you read here is tied to your name or followed from site to site. The rest of this page lists exactly what happens, so you can check it.

## What does the site collect?

Page counts, and nothing else on our side. The site loads one script that does not come from `selfcustodylabs.com`: the Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon, from `static.cloudflareinsights.com`. For each page view it reports the page's address without any query string, the page you came from, your browser's user agent and how long the page took to load, and Cloudflare shows us totals: page views, visits, top pages, referring sites, countries, browsers and load times. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics collects no personal data, uses no cookies or local storage and does not fingerprint visitors, so it cannot recognise you on a later visit or on another site.

There is no tracking pixel, no advertising, no embedded social media and no third-party font. You can confirm this in your browser's network panel: apart from the beacon, every request goes to `selfcustodylabs.com`.

## What does the hosting provider see?

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages receives your IP address, the page you asked for, and your browser's user agent, because it cannot send a page without them. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which keeps those request logs under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). We do not receive or read those logs; the page counts above are the only figures we see.

If you would rather not reveal your IP address, the site works in Tor Browser.

## What is stored in your browser?

Two small values in your browser's local storage, set by the site software and never sent anywhere: whether you closed the announcement bar, and the colour theme. No cookies are set.

## What happens when you email us?

If you write to selfcustodylabs@proton.me, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply and to fix whatever you reported. We never share it.

## Links to other sites

Guides link to wallet makers, GitHub repositories and other references. Those sites have their own policies, and following a link is your request to them, not ours.

## Changes to this policy

When this page changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the [site's source history on GitHub](https://github.com/selfcustodylabs) shows exactly what changed.
