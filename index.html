Where we stand

Why, and how we decide
Coldcardwarned, not recommended
The 2026 entropy incident drained roughly 1,816 BTC from more than 5,200 addresses through a seed-generation flaw that went undetected for 1,978 days, after a developer's warning about the same code was dismissed in 2025. Coinkite fixed it quickly once draining began. We still point new buyers elsewhere.
SeedSignerour favourite, with a caveat
Stateless, air-gapped, fully verifiable, and built from commodity parts for about $50. It is also not a beginner's device, and we say so on every page that mentions it.
Entropydice first
For any holding you would be upset to lose, generate the seed yourself rather than trusting a vendor's random number generator.
Bitkeycovered, not recommended
It is a real product that people ask about, so it appears in the comparison. It is not what we would set up.
CoinJoinJoinMarket NG with Jam
After the 2024 to 2026 shakeout, the coordinator-free option is the one with nothing to seize. Whirlpool's Ashigaru revival is covered but not recommended.
