Learn Bitcoin self-custody

The Learn section explains the ideas the hands-on guides rely on, in six parts: the fundamentals of bitcoin and self-custody, keys and seed phrases, wallets, transactions, privacy, and nodes. If you are new, start with Start here and read in order; every page also stands on its own, so you can go straight to the one you need.

What does each part cover?​

PartStart withWhat it explains
FundamentalsStart hereWhat bitcoin and self-custody are, exchange risk, threat models, choosing a setup
Keys and seed phrasesPrivate keysHow a random number becomes 24 words, extended keys, derivation paths, passphrases, making a seed with dice
WalletsHardware walletsHardware, software and air-gapped wallets, multisig, and the 2026 Coldcard incident
TransactionsHow transactions workUTXOs, creating, signing and broadcasting a transaction, and fees
PrivacyWhy privacy mattersWhat the blockchain reveals, chain analysis, UTXO management, CoinJoin and PayJoin
NodesWhat is a nodeWhat a node does and why running your own changes what you have to trust

Which order should you read in?​

Start here sets out four reading paths: new to bitcoin, your first wallet, stronger security, and maximum security. Each is a short ordered list of pages. Once the concepts are clear, the hands-on guides cover the setups step by step.
