The Learn section explains the ideas the hands-on guides rely on, in six parts: the fundamentals of bitcoin and self-custody, keys and seed phrases, wallets, transactions, privacy, and nodes. If you are new, start with [Start here](https://selfcustodylabs.com/docs/learn/fundamentals/) and read in order; every page also stands on its own, so you can go straight to the one you need.

## What does each part cover?

| Part | Start with | What it explains |
|---|---|---|
| Fundamentals | [Start here](https://selfcustodylabs.com/docs/learn/fundamentals/) | What bitcoin and self-custody are, exchange risk, threat models, choosing a setup |
| Keys and seed phrases | [Private keys](https://selfcustodylabs.com/docs/learn/keys/intro/) | How a random number becomes 24 words, extended keys, derivation paths, passphrases, making a seed with dice |
| Wallets | [Hardware wallets](https://selfcustodylabs.com/docs/learn/wallets/hardware-wallets/) | Hardware, software and air-gapped wallets, multisig, and the 2026 Coldcard incident |
| Transactions | [How transactions work](https://selfcustodylabs.com/docs/learn/transactions/understanding/) | UTXOs, creating, signing and broadcasting a transaction, and fees |
| Privacy | [Why privacy matters](https://selfcustodylabs.com/docs/learn/privacy/why-privacy-matters/) | What the blockchain reveals, chain analysis, UTXO management, CoinJoin and PayJoin |
| Nodes | [What is a node](https://selfcustodylabs.com/docs/learn/nodes/what-is-node/) | What a node does and why running your own changes what you have to trust |

## Which order should you read in?

[Start here](https://selfcustodylabs.com/docs/learn/fundamentals/#which-reading-path-fits-you) sets out four reading paths: new to bitcoin, your first wallet, stronger security, and maximum security. Each is a short ordered list of pages. Once the concepts are clear, the [hands-on guides](https://selfcustodylabs.com/guides/) cover the setups step by step.
