Security hardening guides

Bitcoin security works in layers, added in order as your holdings and threats grow: a hardware wallet with a tested backup first, then a seed you made with dice, a passphrase, operational security (how you behave and what you say), physical security (where things are kept and what happens under threat), and finally multisig. Most people need only the first few; match the layers to your threat model. These guides assume you already have a working hardware wallet; if not, start with wallet setup.

What are the layers?​

LayerWhat it addsGuide
1. Hardware walletKeys stay on a device that signs; most people stop hereHardware wallet setup
2. A seed made with diceRandomness you can verify instead of trusting the deviceSeed generation with dice
3. PassphraseA second secret, so a found seed phrase is not enoughPassphrases
4. Operational securityHabits that stop you giving yourself awayOperational security
5. Physical securityProtection against theft, disaster and threats to youPhysical security
6. MultisigNo single key, device or place can lose or spend everythingMultisig

What does each guide cover?​

GuideTimeCovers
Seed generation with dice2-4 hoursRolling dice for entropy, converting rolls to bits, calculating the BIP39 checksum, backing up on metal. Why: a device's random number generator can fail silently, as Coldcard's did until 2026, and dice-generated seeds were untouched
Passphrases30 minutesHow a passphrase changes the wallet, making a strong one with dice, testing and backing it up. A forgotten passphrase cannot be reset
Operational security30 minutes to readNot discussing holdings, verifying before trusting, assuming devices are compromised, safe communication, social engineering
Physical security30 minutes to readThe "$5 wrench attack", home security, backup locations, travelling with bitcoin, decoy wallets

In what order should you add them?​

StageWhat to doWho
1A hardware wallet with a proper backupEveryone
2Test the backup by restoring from itEveryone
3Stop discussing your holdings publiclyEveryone
4Add a passphraseMeaningful holdings
5Make your own seed with diceHigh security needs
6Secure storage and home securitySignificant holdings
7MultisigLarge holdings

What mistakes undermine security?​

MistakeWhat it looks like
Security theatreWorrying about exotic threats while ignoring the real ones, which are phishing, malware and social engineering
Complexity beyond competenceA setup you could not recover yourself, which is a trap rather than security
Single points of failureOne seed, one place, one device
Trusting without verifyingBelieving a website instead of checking the address on your device and the signature on the software
Talking too muchEvery person who knows you hold bitcoin widens your exposure
