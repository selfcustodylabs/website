Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Self Custody Labs, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Our Commitment to Privacy​

As a Bitcoin education platform focused on self-custody, we understand the importance of privacy. We collect minimal data and do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Data We Collect​

Analytics Data​

We use Google Analytics to understand how our content is used:

  • Page views, visit counts, and referrer information
  • IP addresses are anonymized (anonymizeIP: true)
  • Used to improve content and user experience

What We Do NOT Collect​

  • Personal information (name, email, etc.) unless you contact us directly
  • Bitcoin addresses or transaction data
  • Wallet information
  • Precise location data
  • Cross-site tracking data

Cookies​

This website does not use tracking cookies. Any cookies present are essential for site functionality (e.g., theme preferences stored locally in your browser).

Third-Party Services​

Our website may link to external resources such as:

  • GitHub repositories
  • Hardware wallet manufacturer websites
  • Bitcoin educational resources

These external sites have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

Data Storage​

  • Analytics data is processed by Google Analytics
  • No personal data is stored on our servers
  • Local storage in your browser may save your preferences (dark mode, etc.)

Your Rights​

You can:

  • Use browser privacy features to block analytics
  • Use ad blockers or privacy extensions
  • Browse with JavaScript disabled (basic functionality will work)
  • Contact us to request information about any data we may have

Contact​

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:

Email: selfcustodylabs@proton.me

Changes to This Policy​

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Self Custody Labs is committed to Bitcoin privacy education. We practice what we teach.
