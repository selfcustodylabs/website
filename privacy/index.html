Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Self Custody Labs runs no analytics, sets no cookies, and has no accounts, forms, comments or newsletter. Reading the site sends nothing about you to us. The rest of this page lists the few things that do happen, so you can check them.

What does the site collect?​

Nothing, on our side. There is no analytics script, no tracking pixel, no advertising, no embedded social media and no third-party font or script loaded from another domain. You can confirm this in your browser's network panel: every request goes to selfcustodylabs.com.

What does the hosting provider see?​

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages receives your IP address, the page you asked for, and your browser's user agent, because it cannot send a page without them. The site is currently hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps those request logs under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or read those logs.

If you would rather not reveal your IP address, the site works in Tor Browser.

What is stored in your browser?​

Two small values in your browser's local storage, set by the site software and never sent anywhere: whether you closed the announcement bar, and the colour theme. No cookies are set.

What happens when you email us?​

If you write to selfcustodylabs@proton.me, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply and to fix whatever you reported. We never share it.

Links to other sites​

Guides link to wallet makers, GitHub repositories and other references. Those sites have their own policies, and following a link is your request to them, not ours.

Changes to this policy​

When this page changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the site's source history on GitHub shows exactly what changed.

Contact​

Questions about this policy: selfcustodylabs@proton.me
